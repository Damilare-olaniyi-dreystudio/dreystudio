How we work

How we build your website.

Nine clear steps take your project from understanding the business to launch and ongoing improvement.

01
Step 01

Learn about your business

We learn what you offer, who you serve and what you want the website to help people do. This gives the project a clear starting point.

02
Step 02

Set goals for the site

We agree what a useful result looks like, whether that means more enquiries, online sales, clearer information or a better customer experience.

03
Step 03

Plan the pages customers need

We plan the page list, navigation and main customer journeys so visitors can find services, proof and contact details without confusion.

04
Step 04

Prepare useful content

We organize the words, images and business information people need to understand your offer and make a confident decision.

05
Step 05

Design the experience

We create a visual direction and page layouts that make your business feel credible and help customers move through the site naturally.

06
Step 06

Build and set up features

We develop the responsive website and set up the agreed tools, forms, store features or content controls your team needs.

07
Step 07

Prepare the pages for Google

We set clear page titles, descriptions and headings, organize internal links and check the foundations that help search engines understand the site.

08
Step 08

Test the important details

We review the website on different screens and check key links, forms and page behavior before it is ready for visitors.

09
Step 09

Launch and keep improving

We prepare the site to go live, check the main routes and can continue helping with updates, visibility and improvements as your business grows.
