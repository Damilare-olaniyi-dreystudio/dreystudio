### Post 2 — Case study

For the Nexus Construction website, the central challenge was clarity.

A construction business needs to make its services, completed work and next step easy to understand. The project therefore focused on:

- presenting the business category quickly
- giving services a clear place
- using project work as proof
- keeping the enquiry path visible
- carrying the experience across smaller screens

Good website development starts with the visitor’s first question: “Can this business help me?”

See the project: https://www.dreystudio.ng/work/Nexus-Construction/

### Post 3 — Educational

Before hiring a website developer, ask five questions:

1. Can I see relevant live work?
2. Who owns the domain, hosting and source files?
3. What exactly is included in the scope?
4. What SEO and mobile work is included?
5. What happens after launch?

A low quote is not automatically a good quote if it leaves out the work your business needs.

The right website partner should make the scope, ownership, timeline and support clear before development begins.
