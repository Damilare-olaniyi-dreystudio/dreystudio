Our story

Understand the business. Then build for it.

Drey Studio is a digital studio based in Ibadan, Oyo State. We bring website development, UI/UX design, SEO and graphic design together to help businesses make their online presence more useful.

Every project has a different context: what the business offers, who it needs to reach, what customers need to know and what the website should help them do. We use those answers to shape the structure and design before moving into development.

Our work spans business websites, online stores and digital products for clients in Nigeria and beyond. The goal is a clear, responsive experience that supports the real work of the business.

We bring strategy, design and development into one conversation, so the final experience feels connected from the first screen to the next step.

Meet the founder

Damilare Olaniyi, founder of Drey Studio.

Damilare founded Drey Studio to help businesses make clearer, more useful experiences for the web. He leads the work from early structure and interface decisions through development and launch.

His approach starts with what the business does, who it needs to reach, what information people need and what a useful next action looks like. From there, the work brings together structure, UI/UX, development, performance and search considerations.

What we care about

Useful work is measured by what it helps people do.

Clear user experience

People should understand the offer, find important information and see the next step.

Performance and mobile experience

Responsive behavior and sensible loading are part of the design, not final adjustments.
