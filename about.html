01 / Studio

Who We Are

Drey Studio is a website development and digital design studio focused on helping businesses establish a stronger digital presence.

The studio works with businesses that need a clear, useful place to explain what they do, present their offer and guide visitors toward the next step. That can mean a business website, an online store, a custom web application or a redesign of an existing site.

The philosophy is straightforward: understand the business first, then make the experience clear, responsive and practical to maintain.

02 / Founder

The Person Behind the Studio

Damilare Olaniyi

Founder / Website Developer

Damilare Olaniyi is the founder of Drey Studio and a website developer focused on building websites that help businesses attract customers and grow online.

His approach starts with the client’s actual business needs: what the business does, who it needs to reach, what information people need and what a useful next action looks like. From there, the work brings together structure, UI/UX, development, performance and search considerations.

The aim is not to make a page that only looks finished. It is to create a digital experience that makes sense for the business and the people it serves.

04 / Process

How We Work

Projects are approached deliberately. Each stage helps the next decision become clearer, from the first understanding of the business through the work that keeps a site useful after launch.

01

Discovery

Understand the business, audience, goals and requirements.

02

Research

Review the market, context and references that should shape the experience.

03

Wireframing

Plan the structure, content hierarchy and important user paths.

04

Design

Turn the structure into a considered visual interface.

05

Development

Build the responsive website or digital product around the agreed direction.

06

Testing

Check the experience across devices, interactions and important page states.

07

Launch

Prepare the finished work to go online as a useful business asset.

08

Support

Continue with maintenance, hosting setup or speed optimization when needed.

05 / Principles

What We Care About

Performance

Pages should load with focus and avoid making people wait for the useful parts.

Mobile experience

Responsive behavior is part of the design, not a final adjustment.

Clear user experience

People should be able to understand the offer and find the next step.

SEO

Useful content and sound technical foundations help the right people find the business.

Accessibility

Interfaces should remain understandable and usable for more people.

Business goals

Design and development decisions should serve what the business actually needs.

Maintainable development

A finished website should be practical to update, support and build on.
