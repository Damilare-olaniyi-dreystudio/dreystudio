# Location landing-page research notes

## Sources consulted

- Ogun State Government: https://ogunstate.gov.ng/
  - Confirms the Ogun State context, but the accessible page did not provide enough city-specific business detail for strong Abeokuta claims. Copy therefore avoids unsupported market, demand, or sector assertions.
- Enugu North Local Government: https://enugunorthlga.en.gov.ng/about-us-parent/our-team/
  - Describes Enugu North as a political, economic and cultural centre and identifies trade, technology, hospitality, manufacturing, education, transportation and services as active sectors. It also references Ogbete Main Market, Aria Market, Artisan Market, Coal Camp Auto Spare Parts Market and Polo Park Mall.
- Edo State Government: https://edostate.gov.ng/
  - Describes Edo as a state with economic potential in agriculture and manufacturing, plus technology/data-centre, heritage, culture, education and infrastructure themes. Copy uses these as broad context only and does not claim specific Benin City demand or rankings.
- Drey Studio article: https://dreystudio.ng/blog/professional-business-website-nigeria/
  - Supports the site’s existing messaging around clear positioning, mobile responsiveness, simple navigation, trust signals, calls to action, SEO foundations, reliability and scalability.

## Editorial constraints applied

- No keyword-volume claims were used.
- No local offices, employees, testimonials, partnerships, addresses or client projects were invented.
- Drey Studio is described as based in Ibadan and serving the target cities remotely.
- City-specific content focuses on the business types and website jobs named in the task brief, with restrained local context.
