How We Do It

Our 9-Step Process for Better Websites

Every Drey Studio project moves from business understanding to launch with structure, clarity and search visibility considered from the start.

Step 01

Business & Audience Discovery

We learn what your business offers, how it creates value, who you want to reach and what the website needs to help people do next. This gives the project a clear direction before we make decisions about pages, words or visuals.

Step 02

SEO & Keyword Direction

We identify the questions and searches your customers use, then connect them to useful pages and clear content. That helps us organize the site around real customer intent instead of filling it with vague marketing language.

Step 03

Website Structure & UX

We plan the pages, navigation, key actions and customer journey before development, so the experience stays easy to understand. Wireframes help us test the structure early and prevent important information from getting buried.

Step 04

Content Management Setup

When your project needs editable content, we plan a practical setup for managing pages, products, posts or enquiries. The goal is to make everyday updates practical while keeping the content structure consistent and easy for visitors to use.

Step 05

Technical SEO Foundations

We pay attention to page titles, descriptions, headings, image details, links, performance and other technical foundations. We also make the page structure easier for search engines to crawl and easier for people to understand on Nigerian mobile connections.

Step 06

Security & Quality Checks

We review responsive layouts, forms, links, accessibility basics, loading states and important page states before the website is ready to launch. This is where we catch small issues that can make a polished website feel unreliable.

Step 07

Launch & Route Review

We prepare the finished site for its live domain and review key routes, forms and links so visitors can move through it confidently. Where a redesign is involved, we also pay attention to important existing URLs and redirect planning.

Step 08

Search Measurement Setup

We can prepare the measurement and indexing basics that help you understand how people discover the website and what to improve next. This gives you a more useful view of search visibility than guessing from occasional visits or enquiries.

Step 09

Local Visibility & Ongoing Support

After launch, we can help improve local visibility, update important pages and keep the website useful as your business changes. Support can include content updates, technical improvements and practical SEO work when your project needs continued attention.
