How Much Does a Web Designer Cost in Nigeria?

There is no single reliable price for a website. Costs depend on the number of pages, functionality, design depth, technology or CMS, e-commerce requirements, integrations, content, SEO, hosting, maintenance and the amount of project management required.

For a useful comparison, read our guide to how much a website costs in Nigeria in 2026. If you are planning a store, also review our guide to e-commerce website costs in Nigeria. A provider should be able to explain what is included, what is excluded and what happens after launch.

What changes a quote?

Custom UI/UX, content migration, customer accounts, payment gateways, product variants, dashboards, third-party APIs, multilingual content, accessibility, analytics, technical SEO and post-launch support all change the amount of work. A lower quote is not automatically better if it leaves out the work your business needs.

Web Designer vs Web Developer: What’s the Difference?

For a business owner, the difference is easiest to understand as a sequence. A successful website needs both decisions about experience and decisions about implementation.

  1. Discovery: clarify the audience, goals, constraints and success measures.
  2. Information architecture: organise pages, content and user journeys.
  3. Wireframes: establish layout and hierarchy before visual polish.
  4. UI design: create the interface, visual system, components and responsive states.
  5. Frontend development: turn the approved interface into an accessible responsive website.
  6. Backend development: add data, authentication, business logic and integrations where required.
  7. CMS/e-commerce: connect publishing, products, payments and administration.
  8. SEO: build technical, content and measurement foundations for discovery.
  9. Testing: check devices, browsers, forms, performance, accessibility and content.
  10. Launch: configure hosting, domains, analytics, redirects and backups.
  11. Maintenance: keep software, content, security and performance healthy after launch.

Some people cover several of these roles; others work in a team. The important thing is to know which responsibilities are included in your project.

10 Things to Check Before Hiring a Nigerian Web Designer

  1. Ask to see a recent live portfolio, not only screenshots.
  2. Clarify whether the work is custom, adapted from a template or built from a theme.
  3. Test mobile responsiveness on more than one phone size.
  4. Confirm website, domain, hosting, source-code and account ownership.
  5. Ask what SEO work is included and what is outside scope.
  6. Confirm post-launch support, maintenance and response expectations.
  7. Agree the payment structure and milestones in writing.
  8. List every page, feature, integration and deliverable.
  9. Set a realistic timeline with review and approval points.
  10. Look for relevant previous client feedback and verify it where possible.

Should You Hire a Freelancer or a Web Design Agency?

Neither choice is automatically better. A freelancer may be a good fit for a focused website with a small team, direct communication and a manageable scope. An agency may be useful when the project requires several disciplines, more delivery capacity, a larger content or migration effort, or a defined support model.

The correct choice depends on project size, complexity, budget, required disciplines and support requirements. Ask both types of provider the same questions about scope, ownership, communication, availability, quality assurance and what happens if the project changes.
