How Much Does Website Design Cost in Ibadan?

A practical guide to what affects the price, what a useful business website should include, and how to compare a developer’s quote before you commit.

Written by Damilare Olaniyi, Founder of Drey Studio · Published October 26, 2026

If you are planning a website for your business in Ibadan, the first quote you receive may not look anything like the next one. That does not always mean that one developer is being unfair. Often, the two quotes describe different projects.

The short answer

Website design costs in Ibadan depend on the type of website, the number of pages, the level of custom design, the features required, and what happens after launch. A simple business website and an online store are not the same project, so their budgets should not be compared as if they are.

Before you compare prices, compare the work each quote includes.

Why do website quotes differ?

One business may need a simple website with a homepage, service pages, an About page, and a contact route. Another may need product pages, online payments, a booking system, a blog, or a private dashboard.

Both businesses may say, “I need a website,” but they are asking for different levels of planning, design, development, and testing.

The price is usually affected by five questions:

  • How much information does the website need to present?
  • Does the business need a custom design or a simpler starting point?
  • What should visitors be able to do on the website?
  • Who will prepare the writing, images, and other content?
  • Who will maintain the website after it goes live?

What affects website design cost in Ibadan?

1. The number of pages

A small business website may need only a few important pages. A company with several services, locations, industries, or audiences may need a deeper structure. More pages mean more planning, writing, design, development, and testing.

2. The type of website

A landing page, a professional company website, an online store, and a custom web application all require different work. The number of pages matters, but what the website needs to do matters even more.

3. The design approach

A ready-made template can be a reasonable choice for a small project. A custom website takes more time because the layout and user experience are shaped around the business, its audience, and its brand.

4. The features

A contact form is relatively simple. Payments, product catalogues, booking, customer accounts, dashboards, and connections to other systems require more development and testing.

5. The content

If your page copy, images, logo, and brand materials are ready, the project can move more smoothly. If the developer must also organise your information, write the content, source images, or create graphics, that becomes part of the project scope.

6. Search and post-launch support

Some websites are built to go live and stop there. Others are prepared with clearer page structure, useful titles, mobile considerations, and room for future updates. You should also ask whether support, maintenance, and content changes are included after launch.

What should a professional website project include?

A useful website project should be clear about its deliverables before work starts. Depending on the scope, you should ask whether the proposal covers:

  • Page planning and content structure
  • Custom or template-based design
  • Mobile-responsive layouts
  • Website copy and image preparation
  • Contact forms or WhatsApp contact routes
  • Basic search-friendly structure
  • Testing before launch
  • Domain and hosting setup
  • Revisions and approval stages
  • Ownership and access after handover
  • Post-launch support
Ask for the scope in writing.
It is easier to compare two quotes when you can see the pages, features, content responsibilities, revision limits, launch work, and support included in each one.

What costs continue after the website is built?

The development fee may not be the full cost of owning a website. You may also need to plan for:

  • Domain renewal
  • Hosting renewal
  • Business email
  • Payment processing fees
  • Content updates
  • Maintenance and technical support
  • Ongoing search or marketing work

These costs vary by provider and by the kind of website you operate. A simple business website may need little attention. An online store or application may need more regular updates and support.

How to compare website developers before paying

Do not compare quotes by price alone. Ask each developer the same questions:

  • What pages are included?
  • Is the design custom or based on a template?
  • Who writes the content?
  • Is mobile design included?
  • What features are included?
  • How many revisions are allowed?
  • Who owns the domain and hosting account?
  • What happens if the website needs changes later?
  • Can I see relevant work for businesses like mine?

A good developer should be able to explain the work in language you understand. You should not need to guess what you are paying for.

What should an Ibadan business think about first?

Start with the business problem, not the technology.

A school may need parents to find important information quickly. A real estate company may need visitors to explore properties and request inspections. A professional service may need a clear explanation of its expertise and an easy way to make an enquiry. An online shop may need customers to browse, pay, and receive order information.

Those goals lead to different page structures. That is why a useful website conversation should begin with your audience, your offer, and the action you want visitors to take.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost in Ibadan?

The cost depends on the type of website, the number of pages, the design approach, the features, the content, and the support required. A clear quote should be based on the actual scope rather than a vague website category.

How long does it take to build a business website?

The timeline depends on the size of the website, how quickly content is supplied, the number of review stages, and the features required. A developer should confirm the expected timeline after understanding the scope.

Do I need to pay for hosting and a domain separately?

Sometimes they are included for an initial period, and sometimes they are charged separately. Ask what is included in the first quote and what you will need to renew later.

Can a website include WhatsApp?

Yes. A website can include a WhatsApp contact route where it fits the business and the visitor journey. The exact placement should support the page goal without distracting from the main content.

The right website starts with the right scope

There is no single website price that is right for every business. A small service provider may need a focused website with a strong enquiry route. A larger company may need more pages, a deeper content structure, or integrations that require additional development.

The useful question is not only, “How much does a website cost?” It is also, “What should this website help my business do, and what work is needed to make that happen?”
