Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to develop a website?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the project. Most business websites are completed within a few weeks once all content, requirements and project details have been provided.

What types of websites do you create?

Drey Studio builds business, corporate, portfolio, landing-page, e-commerce, startup, real estate and logistics websites, along with other digital products shaped around project requirements.

What do you need from me to get started?

Useful starting information includes the business context, goals, website content, examples you like and any specific features you need included.

Do you redesign existing websites?

Yes. Existing websites can be redesigned when they feel outdated, difficult to use or no longer represent the business properly.

Can you help with website content and structure?

Yes. The project can consider page structure, user journeys, content hierarchy and calls to action so the website is easier for visitors to understand and use.

Do you build e-commerce websites?

Yes. Drey Studio creates e-commerce experiences that present products clearly and support a practical browsing and buying journey.

Do you provide website maintenance after launch?

Ongoing support can include website maintenance, hosting setup and speed optimization where the project requires it.

Can you improve an existing website's speed?

Speed optimization is an available ongoing service. The work can focus on practical improvements such as image handling, page structure and the assets a website loads.
