Planning your investment

What moves a project from one package to another?

The price depends on the amount of work needed to make the finished website useful for your customers and manageable for your team.

01

Pages and content

A focused set of pages takes less planning and production than a large site with many distinct services, audiences, or content types.

02

Online selling

Product catalogues, categories, payment setup, and shopping journeys add work beyond a standard business website.

03

Custom features

Dashboards, integrations, booking flows, and other business-specific tools are scoped around how your team needs them to work.

04

Existing website

A redesign may involve content migration, technical repairs, or a new structure, depending on the condition of the current site.
