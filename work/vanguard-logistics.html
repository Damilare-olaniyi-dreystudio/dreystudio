Project introduction

A clearer digital front door for logistics.

Vanguard Logistics needed a professional business website where prospective customers could discover the company, understand its offering and decide how to begin a conversation.

I designed and developed the website around that purpose. The experience introduces the logistics brand, gives its main service areas visible routes in the navigation and keeps contact and Get Started actions close to the journey.

The result is a focused logistics company website that presents the business with more structure than a single promotional message can provide.

Strategic approach

Introduce, explain, reassure, connect.

The website structure follows a practical business-user journey instead of treating the homepage as an isolated visual statement.

01 / Introduce

The hero establishes Vanguard’s logistics category and presents a clear first action through the global logistics message and Explore Our Network CTA.

02 / Explain

Global Network, Transportation, Warehousing, Supply Chain and Technology are visible navigation routes that help visitors understand the website’s information architecture.

03 / Reassure

Impact, Testimonials and FAQ routes give the business space to communicate credibility, context and answers before contact.

04 / Connect

Contact and Get Started actions provide a visible path for a prospective customer who wants to continue the conversation.
