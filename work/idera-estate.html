Project introduction

A property website built to move buyers forward.

IDERA is a residential real estate website project designed around the questions prospective buyers ask before they enquire.

The experience gives visitors clearer ways to discover homes, compare the character of different properties and understand what to do next. Featured homes, locations, property types and inspection actions work together as one property-search journey.

For the business, the objective is practical: present the portfolio with more clarity, build confidence before contact and create more useful opportunities for a buyer to request an inspection or start a conversation.

See how this approach connects to Drey Studio’s website development service.

The business challenge

A property catalogue needs to answer questions before the enquiry.

Real estate visitors need more than attractive photography. They need a clear way to find relevant options, understand the details and decide whether an inspection is worth requesting.

Make discovery easier

Give visitors clear entry points into properties instead of making them search through an undifferentiated catalogue.

Organise the decision

Present location, property type, budget and bedroom choices in a way that helps visitors narrow their options.

Build confidence before contact

Use property imagery, context and a consistent brand presentation to make the portfolio easier to take seriously.

Keep the next step visible

Connect property browsing to inspection and enquiry actions so interest does not stop at the gallery.

The strategy

Discover, explore, evaluate, enquire.

The structure follows the property-search journey so each stage gives the visitor a reason to continue and the business a clearer path to a useful conversation.

01 / Discover

The hero message, location context and featured properties tell visitors what IDERA offers and where the portfolio is focused.

02 / Explore

Featured homes, property types, locations and lifestyle groupings create several practical ways into the collection.

03 / Evaluate

Property imagery and organised details help visitors understand whether a home fits what they are looking for.

04 / Enquire

Explore Properties and Book an Inspection actions keep the next step visible when interest becomes specific.

The solution

Features connected to the property decision.

The work is more than a collection of screens. Each part of the interface supports a specific question a buyer or agent needs the website to answer.

Property discovery

The homepage presents featured properties, locations, categories and a search-style entry point so visitors can begin with the route that feels most relevant to them.

Buyer confidence

Large property imagery, location context and a consistent visual system help visitors understand the portfolio before they decide whether to enquire.

Enquiry conversion

Property-focused actions such as Explore Properties and Book an Inspection connect interest to a clear next step rather than leaving the visitor at the end of a gallery.

Mobile property search

The responsive design keeps the main message, property browsing and action hierarchy usable when visitors are searching from smaller screens.

Sales conversation support

When property context and preferences are visible before contact, the website can give a prospective buyer and the real estate team a more useful starting point for discussion.

Business outcomes

Business outcomes the website is designed to support.

These are the business objectives behind the implementation. They describe the value the experience is intended to create; they are not presented as measured performance results.

Make property discovery easier

Objective: Help prospective buyers find relevant homes faster. Design decision: Use featured properties, locations, categories and a search-style opening. Intended value: More visitors can reach a relevant property with less uncertainty.

Build buyer confidence

Objective: Help visitors understand what they are considering. Design decision: Give property imagery, location context and presentation a consistent place in the journey. Intended value: Visitors can evaluate the portfolio before making contact.

Encourage property enquiries

Objective: Turn interest in a specific home into a possible conversation. Design decision: Keep Explore Properties and Book an Inspection actions visible. Intended value: Interested visitors have a clearer route to the real estate team.

Improve the mobile experience

Objective: Keep property browsing useful on smaller screens. Design decision: Carry the hierarchy, imagery and action paths into a responsive layout. Intended value: Mobile visitors can continue from discovery to the next action without needing a desktop.

Support informed sales conversations

Objective: Give prospective buyers useful context before they enquire. Design decision: Organise property and location information around the browsing journey. Intended value: Conversations can begin with a clearer understanding of the visitor’s interest.
