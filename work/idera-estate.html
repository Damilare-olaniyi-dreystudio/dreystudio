Overview

A better way to find where you belong.

IDERA is a modern residential property brand built around the way people actually search for a home.

The website brings featured properties, locations, categories and lifestyle browsing into one calm, premium experience. It gives the visual story of each home room to breathe while keeping the next step close at hand.

From the first scroll to booking an inspection, every part of the journey is designed to feel considered rather than complicated.

The challenge

Property discovery should not feel like work.

A real-estate catalogue can show plenty of homes and still leave people unsure where to begin.

Make the first impression count

Present IDERA as a premium residential brand before the visitor has to compare a single property.

Give people more than one way in

Let visitors start with a featured home, a location, a property type or the way they want to live.

Keep the decision moving

Move naturally from browsing to property details, then from interest to inspection.

Stay clear at every size

Carry the same calm hierarchy across desktop, tablet and mobile screens.

The approach

The interface does the explaining.

Instead of adding more words, the experience uses scale, imagery and a clear sequence to help people decide what matters next.

Discover

A strong opening image and focused message establish the feeling of the brand immediately.

Explore

Featured homes, locations, categories and lifestyle cues make browsing feel natural.

Understand

Property information is paced so visitors can build confidence without being overwhelmed.

Act

Explore Properties and Book an Inspection stay visible as useful, low-friction next steps.

Typography

Arial

Arial, Helvetica and sans-serif are used across headings, navigation and body copy for a direct, readable interface. The strong display scale gives the property experience confidence while the simple letterforms keep it easy to scan.
