Project introduction

A fashion storefront built around discovery.

Ewa Fashion needed a dedicated online destination where visitors could understand the brand, browse the collection and move toward a shopping decision.

The interface leads with a clear fashion statement, then gives the visitor practical routes into Shop, Women, Men, Accessories and New Arrivals. Search, wishlist and cart controls are also made visible in the main navigation.

The result is structured as a shopping experience: introduce the collection, make products easier to find, show enough context to support evaluation and keep the next action clear.

The business challenge

A fashion collection needs a clear route to the product.

Online shoppers make quick decisions. The website has to establish the brand, help them find a relevant category and keep product interest moving.

Establish the retail presence

Give Ewa Fashion a dedicated digital storefront that feels distinct and easy to recognise.

Make the collection discoverable

Use clear category routes so shoppers can move into the part of the collection that interests them.

Support product evaluation

Give product imagery, pricing and shopping controls a visible place in the experience.

Keep purchase intent moving

Connect browsing to visible shopping actions without making the interface difficult to scan.
