Project introduction

A logistics platform built around visibility.

TrackPoint brings a courier brand, delivery booking and shipment management into one connected digital product.

The public website explains the service in plain language, gives people a route to book or track a shipment, and creates a professional first impression for both individual and business customers.

Behind that customer-facing layer, the platform is designed to support the people responsible for the delivery journey. Individual customers, business accounts, drivers and administrators each need a different view of the same moving shipment.

The work applies Drey Studio's website development approach to a product where clear information matters at every stage.

The business challenge

Every delivery has more than one person to serve.

A logistics experience has to make a customer feel confident enough to book while giving the delivery team the information needed to keep that shipment moving.

Make delivery services easy to understand

Give first-time visitors a quick explanation of what the service does and a direct route to the action they need.

Keep shipment progress visible

Make tracking a primary customer action, not an afterthought hidden behind a support channel.

Support more than one type of user

Give individuals, businesses, drivers and administrators purpose-built spaces for their part of the delivery process.

Connect the public brand to operations

Bring booking, payment choices, tracking and delivery management into one product story.

The strategy

Understand, book, track, manage.

The website and platform follow the natural order of a delivery, so a customer and the delivery team can each find the information that matters to them.

01

Understand

The homepage introduces the service, customer groups and the value of clearer delivery updates.

02

Book

Customers can begin a delivery, enter shipment details, review an estimated cost and select a payment option.

03

Track

A dedicated tracking route gives people a place to check shipment status and follow delivery progress.

04

Manage

Role-based portals support the hand-off from customer booking to delivery coordination and oversight.

Website experience

A clear route into the service.

The public website makes two important actions visible from the opening screen: book a delivery or track a shipment already in motion.

Design direction

Confident, practical and built for movement.

TrackPoint's own interface uses a calm logistics palette and task-led layout. The case study itself stays inside Drey Studio's dark and purple portfolio system.

Action-led homepage

The opening screen gives booking and tracking equal visual weight, so both new and returning visitors can move forward quickly.

Delivery information that stays readable

Direct navigation, simple labels and generous spacing make a complex service easier to understand without flattening the brand.

Platform experience

One shipment journey, different working views.

TrackPoint's platform is structured for the people who use shipment information in different ways throughout a delivery.

Individual customers

A personal space for booking deliveries, checking shipment progress and managing delivery information.

Business accounts

A business view for organising deliveries and managing shipment activity that supports day-to-day operations.

Delivery drivers

A dedicated operational view for assigned shipments and the delivery work that needs to be completed.

Administrators

An oversight view for managing shipments and the wider flow of delivery activity across the platform.

Delivery workflow

From delivery request to progress update.

The product connects the steps customers see with the information and actions needed to coordinate a delivery behind the scenes.

Shipment creation

The booking journey collects delivery information, gives the customer an estimate and leads into payment selection.

Delivery coordination

Shipment information becomes the shared record managed through the relevant customer, driver and administrator views.

Progress visibility

The tracking experience gives a shipment a visible customer-facing status route after the delivery has been created.

Service confidence

Clear actions and clearer progress information help make a delivery service easier to understand before and after booking.

Responsive experience

The delivery journey stays clear on every screen.

The responsive layouts preserve booking and tracking priorities across desktop, tablet and mobile, where many customers will need fast access to a shipment update.
