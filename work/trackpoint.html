Project introduction

Delivery services, easier to access and manage.

TrackPoint Logistics is a courier brand supported by a digital platform for booking, tracking and managing shipments.

For a customer, the experience starts with a simple need: send something and know where it is. TrackPoint gives people a clear route to start a delivery, understand the cost, choose a payment option and check progress after the shipment is on its way.

The platform also gives the people behind each delivery dedicated views of the same journey. Individuals, businesses, drivers and administrators can each work with the information that matters to them.

The business need

A delivery service has to work for everyone involved.

Customers need a straightforward way to book and track. Delivery teams need a connected view of the information that turns a request into a completed shipment.

Make delivery services easy to use

People should be able to understand the service and start a shipment without hunting for the next step.

Make costs clearer before booking

Delivery details, cost estimates and payment options sit in one practical booking journey.

Keep shipment progress visible

Customers have a direct way to check the status of a delivery once it is in motion.

Support the people doing the work

Businesses, drivers and administrators have focused tools for their different responsibilities around a shipment.

Easier shipment booking

A clearer path to sending a shipment.

The public website gives prospective customers an immediate route into the service: book a delivery or track one already in progress.

A customer can provide shipment details, receive an estimated cost and select a payment option in one flow. That structure reduces the back-and-forth between deciding to send a package and taking the first practical step.

Greater shipment visibility

A shipment should not disappear after booking.

Tracking gives customers a direct way to follow delivery progress and access relevant shipment information.

A visible progress route

Tracking is a primary action on the public website, so customers can return to the information they need without relying on a support conversation.

Clearer customer communication

A dedicated tracking experience gives the courier brand a more transparent way to keep delivery progress accessible after booking.

Supporting different users

The right view for each part of delivery operations.

TrackPoint is built around the fact that a customer, a business, a driver and an administrator each use shipment information differently.

A personal area for managing deliveries, checking shipment progress and reviewing delivery information.

Business accounts

A dedicated business view for organisations managing shipment requirements as part of their day-to-day work.

Delivery drivers

An operational view for accessing assigned deliveries and the work connected to each shipment.

Administrators

An oversight area for managing shipment activity and coordinating operations across the platform.

The outcome

A stronger digital home for TrackPoint Logistics.
