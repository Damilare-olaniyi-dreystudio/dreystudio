Project introduction

A skincare website built around product discovery.

AURA needed a digital presence that could introduce the brand, show the product world clearly and give interested visitors a natural way to continue the conversation.

The experience starts with a strong editorial point of view, then moves visitors through product presentation, brand context and visible contact access. It is designed as a product discovery and enquiry website, not a checkout-led store.

That distinction shaped the work: the website has to make the offering understandable and the next step easy to recognise.

The business challenge

A skincare brand needs clarity before the enquiry.

Visitors should be able to understand what AURA is, what the brand presents and where to go next without having to decode the interface.

Establish the presence

Give AURA a dedicated digital home with a recognisable brand point of view.

Make products easier to find

Keep the collection and product-led story visible from the first interaction.

Explain the offering

Use hierarchy, imagery and content space to help visitors understand what is presented.

Keep contact accessible

Make the route from interest to a product conversation clear and easy to take.
