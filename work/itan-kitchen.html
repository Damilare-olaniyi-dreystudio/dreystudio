Project introduction

A food website with a clear appetite for action.

Itan Kitchen is a restaurant website project built around the way people choose where and what to eat.

The website gives the brand a focused online home: a clear first impression, an easy route into the menu, and visible ways to move from interest to an order or enquiry.

For a food business, the website has to make the offering feel worth exploring before it asks someone to take action. That meant giving food photography room to work, keeping the main routes simple, and making the next step easy to spot.

It also reflects Drey Studio's broader approach to website development: build the visual idea around a real customer journey.

The business challenge

A restaurant needs more than a nice food photo.

The site needed to give a food brand a recognisable digital presence while helping visitors understand the offering and decide what to do next.

Make the brand memorable

Give the restaurant a distinct first impression rather than an interchangeable food-service template.

Bring the menu forward

Make it easier for visitors to find the food offering and understand what is available.

Support an order decision

Keep the move from browsing to an order or contact action direct and easy to understand.

Work well on smaller screens

Keep the main story, menu entry point and customer actions usable when people are browsing on a phone.

The strategy

Arrive, explore, choose, act.

The website is organised around a simple restaurant journey, with each stage answering the next question a potential customer is likely to have.

01

Arrive

A food-led hero introduces the restaurant and sets a clear, warm tone straight away.

02

Explore

Navigation gives visitors direct routes to the menu, offers, group orders and more about the restaurant.

03

Choose

Large food imagery and a menu-focused route help customers move from general interest to a more specific choice.

04

Act

Visible order and contact actions give people a clear path when they are ready to take the next step.

Website experience

The restaurant and the menu share the same stage.

Instead of separating brand story from food discovery, the interface uses the same page hierarchy to make the experience feel connected.

Design direction

Food-led, expressive and easy to move through.

The visual direction lets the restaurant's own presentation do the work, while Drey Studio's case-study environment keeps the project story consistent with the rest of the portfolio.

Design decision

Let the food make the first impression.

Large, appetite-led imagery is balanced with generous space and short copy. The result gives visitors a clear sense of the restaurant before they need to make a decision.

Features and navigation

Built around the questions a customer asks.

The visible navigation and calls to action give the restaurant's customer journey a clear structure.

Menu discovery

Menu is a primary navigation item and the homepage gives it a prominent entry point, helping visitors move quickly into the food offering.

Ordering route

An Order Now action stays visible in the main hero, so the site does not make a ready customer search for the next step.

More ways to browse

About Us, Offers and Group Orders give customers useful routes beyond the standard menu path.

Contact and FAQs

A dedicated Contact & FAQs route gives visitors a clear place to resolve questions before they make a decision.

Responsive experience

One restaurant experience, across the screens people use.

The layout keeps the restaurant's visual character intact across desktop, tablet and mobile, while making the main route into the menu and order journey easy to find.

Project outcome

A clearer digital home for the restaurant.

The completed website gives the brand a dedicated place to tell its story, present the food and make the customer’s next action easier to find.

Itan Kitchen now has a structured restaurant experience that brings its brand presentation, menu discovery, offers, group orders and customer contact routes into one website.

The work is designed to help a potential customer make sense of the restaurant quickly, then move forward when the time is right.
