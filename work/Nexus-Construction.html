Overview

A clearer way to present the work.

Nexus Construction is a project-focused website built around the practical questions a potential client needs answered.

The experience brings the construction category, services and completed projects into one straightforward digital presence. It gives the business a more considered first impression while keeping the path to enquiry visible.

The result is designed to help a visitor understand what Nexus does, see the quality of its work and decide what to do next.

The challenge

Trust starts with clear information.

A construction website has to make the business understandable before a visitor is ready to ask for a conversation.

Explain the offer

Show the work

Support enquiry

Keep the next step close to the information that helps a potential client make a decision.

Stay responsive

Carry the same hierarchy and clarity across desktop, tablet and smaller screens.
