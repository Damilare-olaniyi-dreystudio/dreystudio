Overview

Clarity with atmosphere.

Lumina Estates is a Drey Studio website project for a luxury residential real-estate brand presenting distinctive homes across international markets.

The experience brings architectural imagery, curated properties, location context and lifestyle storytelling into one visual system. It is designed to make premium homes feel desirable without making the information difficult to find.

Visitors can move from the first impression to property discovery, viewing requests and private consultation actions through a clear, editorial path.

The challenge

Luxury, without the friction.

A high-value property website has to do more than display beautiful images. It must create desire, communicate enough detail and keep the next step visible.

Set the tone immediately

Establish Lumina Estates as a confident luxury brand before visitors begin comparing properties.

Make the collection easy to enter

Give visitors clear ways to begin with a featured home, a location, a property type or a lifestyle preference.

Balance image and information

Let architectural photography lead while keeping locations, prices and specifications understandable.

Keep interest moving forward

Make viewing requests and private consultations feel like natural next steps rather than interruptions.

Our approach

Let the properties lead.

The design uses scale, restraint and sequencing to give the property story room to breathe while keeping the browsing journey practical.

Discover

An immersive opening establishes the architectural mood and gives the brand a distinct first impression.

Explore

Featured properties, portfolio groupings and international locations create several clear entry points.

Understand

Property information is structured around the questions a serious buyer or investor needs answered.

Enquire

Viewing and consultation actions remain visible so interest can become a private conversation.

Typography & space

Editorial scale

Oversized typography creates confidence, while generous spacing lets the architecture and property imagery carry the emotional weight. Structured layouts keep the experience easy to scan.
